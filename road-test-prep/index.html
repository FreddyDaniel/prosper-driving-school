Prepared, not just practiced

The NJ road test is short, so small mistakes matter. Our road test prep lessons recreate the test: we run through the maneuvers, score you like an examiner, and fix the habits that cost points.

Most Bergen County drivers test at the NJ MVC road test site in Lodi (8 Mill St, off Garibaldi Ave), and some test in Wayne (481 Route 46 West). Road tests are by appointment only through the NJ MVC.

  • Mock road test with clear feedback
  • Three-point turn (K-turn), parallel parking and backing
  • Stop signs, right-of-way, signaling and mirror checks
  • Warm-up lesson right before your test

Car rental for your road test

No car, or your car isn't road-test ready? The vehicle you test in needs valid registration and insurance and working lights, signals, brakes and horn. Our road test package can include use of our vehicle for the test. Call to check availability for your date.

Bring the right paperwork

Drivers permitted under 21 on or after February 1, 2025 must bring the BA-CSD Certification of Supervised Driving, confirming 50 hours of practice including 10 at night. See our NJ road test checklist and what to bring to the MVC.

FAQ

Questions we hear

Can I rent your car for the road test?

Yes. Our road test package can include use of our vehicle and a warm-up lesson. Call 551-999-6131 to check availability.

Where are the road test sites near Bergen County?

The NJ MVC lists road test sites in Lodi (8 Mill St, off Garibaldi Ave) and Wayne (481 Route 46 West), among others. Tests are by appointment only.

What maneuvers are on the NJ road test?

Expect basics like starting, stopping, signaling and turning, along with maneuvers such as the three-point (K) turn, parallel parking and backing. Check the NJ MVC for current details.

How many prep lessons do I need?

It depends on your experience. Many students benefit from one or two mock-test lessons plus a warm-up on test day.
