Learning to drive in Englewood

Englewood's downtown, centered on Palisade Avenue and Grand Avenue, is full of the situations road tests and real life care about: angled sight lines at intersections, pedestrians at crosswalks, delivery trucks double-parked, and on-street parking that rewards a smooth parallel park. Englewood Hospital and the busy medical offices around it add steady traffic throughout the day.

Just outside downtown, Route 4 runs through the south end of Englewood and Route 9W and the Palisades Interstate Parkway are nearby to the east. That makes Englewood a convenient base for students who want to progress from residential streets to commercial districts to divided highways, all within the same lesson block.

Local roads we practice

  • Palisade Avenue and Grand Avenue downtown
  • On-street parallel parking
  • Route 4 through south Englewood
  • Engle Street and Van Nostrand Avenue

Road test for Englewood drivers

Englewood drivers commonly test at the NJ MVC road test site in Lodi (8 Mill St, off Garibaldi Ave). Englewood students most often test in Lodi, a straightforward drive via Route 4 West and Route 17 South or local roads. NJ MVC road tests are by appointment only; schedule through the NJ MVC. Our road test prep includes mock tests and can include use of our car.

Englewood driving lessons FAQ

Do you teach parallel parking in Englewood?

Yes. Parallel parking is required on the NJ road test, and Englewood's on-street parking makes for realistic practice.

Are lessons available for adults in Englewood?

Absolutely. We teach adult beginners, returning drivers, seniors who want a refresher, and nervous drivers.
