Learning to drive in River Edge

River Edge is a quiet borough with a few busy roads that new drivers need to learn. Route 4 cuts across the southern end of town, Kinderkamack Road runs north-south past shops and businesses, and NJ Transit's Pascack Valley Line runs along the town, so drivers need to know how to approach railroad crossings safely.

Residential neighborhoods off Kinderkamack Road and Continental Avenue are ideal for early lessons, and the Hackensack River on the town's eastern edge adds a few bridge crossings. River Edge families often take the teen course with us, then return for parent-requested practice sessions on Route 4 and road test prep.

Local roads we practice

  • Kinderkamack Road
  • Route 4
  • Railroad crossings on the Pascack Valley Line

Road test for River Edge drivers

River Edge drivers commonly test at the NJ MVC road test site in Lodi (8 Mill St, off Garibaldi Ave). River Edge drivers usually test in Lodi via Route 4 West and Route 17 South. NJ MVC road tests are by appointment only; schedule through the NJ MVC. Our road test prep includes mock tests and can include use of our car.

River Edge driving lessons FAQ

Do you teach how to cross railroad tracks safely?

Yes. Railroad crossing safety is part of our lessons and is covered in the NJ Driver Manual and knowledge test.

Can parents ask for specific skills to be practiced?

Yes. Let us know what you've noticed during practice driving and we'll work on it.
