Why Bergen County is a unique place to learn

Bergen County packs a lot of driving into a small area. Route 4 and Route 17 carry heavy commuter and shopping traffic, often with jughandles instead of left turns. The Garden State Parkway runs through the western half of the county, I-80 and Route 46 cross the south, and I-95 and the New Jersey Turnpike lead to the George Washington Bridge in Fort Lee.

Between those highways are dozens of compact towns with their own downtowns, school zones, railroad crossings and tight parking. Learning here means practicing variety, which is exactly what our lessons are designed for.

Road test sites for Bergen County

  • Lodi: 8 Mill St (off Garibaldi Ave), Lodi, NJ 07644
  • Wayne: 481 Route 46 West, Wayne, NJ 07470

Road tests are by appointment only, Monday through Friday, per the NJ MVC. Check the MVC for current hours and availability.

FAQ

Bergen County FAQ

Which Bergen County towns do you serve?

We serve towns across Bergen County, including Hackensack, Teaneck, Paramus, Fort Lee, Englewood, Hasbrouck Heights, Lodi, Bergenfield, Ridgefield Park, Garfield, River Edge, Maywood, Fair Lawn, Ridgewood, Lyndhurst, Rutherford, plus nearby North Jersey communities.

Where do Bergen County drivers take the road test?

The NJ MVC road test site in Lodi (8 Mill St) is the closest for most of Bergen County; Wayne (481 Route 46 West) is also used, especially in western Bergen.

Do Bergen County's blue laws affect driving?

Bergen County's blue laws keep most retail stores closed on Sundays, so shopping corridors like Route 4 and Route 17 in Paramus are much quieter on Sundays.
