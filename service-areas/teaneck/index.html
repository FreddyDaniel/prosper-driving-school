Learning to drive in Teaneck

Teaneck combines long, leafy residential streets with some of the busiest commuter corridors in Bergen County. Cedar Lane's shops and on-street parking, the traffic signals along Teaneck Road and Queen Anne Road, and the school zones scattered across town give new drivers plenty to practice at a comfortable pace.

Teaneck is also boxed in by major highways: Route 4 runs along its southern edge and I-95 and the New Jersey Turnpike cut through the east side. We use the calmer interior streets to build fundamentals, then move to Route 4's on-ramps and jughandle-style turns once students have the control and confidence to handle faster traffic. Students near Fairleigh Dickinson University's Metropolitan Campus can also practice around River Road and the Hackensack River.

Local roads we practice

  • Cedar Lane and its on-street parking
  • Teaneck Road and Queen Anne Road signals
  • Route 4 merges and jughandles
  • River Road along the Hackensack River

Road test for Teaneck drivers

Teaneck drivers commonly test at the NJ MVC road test site in Lodi (8 Mill St, off Garibaldi Ave). Most Teaneck students take their road test in Lodi, which is easy to reach via Route 80 or local roads through Hackensack. NJ MVC road tests are by appointment only; schedule through the NJ MVC. Our road test prep includes mock tests and can include use of our car.

Teaneck driving lessons FAQ

Are lessons available for Teaneck high school students?

Yes. We work with teens 16 and older who need the state-required 6 hours of behind-the-wheel training to get their special learner permit, as well as teens preparing for the road test.

What are the toughest roads for new drivers in Teaneck?

Route 4 merges and the busy stretches of Cedar Lane and Teaneck Road tend to be the most challenging. We build up to them gradually.

How do I book driving lessons in Teaneck?

Call 551-999-6131 or request a lesson online. We're based at 131 Main Street, Suite 168, Hackensack, NJ 07601, and serve Teaneck and the rest of Bergen County.
