North Jersey driving, mastered

North Jersey is a dense web of highways: the Garden State Parkway, I-80, I-95 and the New Jersey Turnpike, Route 4, Route 17, Route 3, Route 46 and Route 208. Add winter snow and ice, and a new driver needs real skill, not just a passed test.

Our lessons are based in Hackensack, near the center of Bergen County. That puts many North Jersey communities within reach, especially those near the Bergen County line.

What North Jersey drivers learn with us

  • Merging on short ramps and handling jughandles
  • Toll plazas and lane choice on the Garden State Parkway
  • Truck traffic on I-80 and the Turnpike
  • Winter driving on hills and bridges
  • Downtown parking and pedestrian awareness
FAQ

North Jersey FAQ

Do you serve towns outside Bergen County?

Our focus is Bergen County, but we can often accommodate nearby North Jersey communities. Call 551-999-6131 to ask about your town.

Which North Jersey MVC road test sites are closest?

For most of our students, Lodi (8 Mill St) and Wayne (481 Route 46 West). The NJ MVC also lists Randolph and Newark among its road test locations.

Ready to get behind the wheel?

Call us or request a lesson online and we'll help you choose the right plan.
