Learning to drive in Paramus

Paramus is where Bergen County's retail corridors meet. Route 4 and Route 17 cross in the middle of town, the Garden State Parkway runs along the west side, and the big shopping centers along both highways (including Westfield Garden State Plaza and Paramus Park) generate heavy weekday and Saturday traffic. Learning to drive confidently here means mastering highway-side driveways, jughandles, and right-lane merges with traffic that's moving fast.

Paramus also has a feature found in few places in the country: Bergen County's blue laws keep most retail stores closed on Sundays, so Sunday traffic on the highways feels very different from Saturday. We use that variety on purpose. Quieter residential neighborhoods off Midland Avenue and Paramus Road are perfect for first lessons, and the highway corridors become advanced practice once a driver is ready.

Local roads we practice

  • Route 4 and Route 17 interchange
  • Garden State Parkway entrances
  • Jughandles along Route 17
  • Residential streets off Paramus Road and Midland Avenue

Road test for Paramus drivers

Paramus drivers commonly test at the NJ MVC road test site in Lodi (8 Mill St, off Garibaldi Ave). Lodi is the closest MVC road test site for most Paramus drivers, reachable by Route 17 South. NJ MVC road tests are by appointment only; schedule through the NJ MVC. Our road test prep includes mock tests and can include use of our car.

FAQ

Paramus driving lessons FAQ

Why does Paramus traffic feel different on Sundays?

Bergen County's blue laws keep most retail stores closed on Sundays, so the Route 4 and Route 17 shopping corridors are much quieter than on Saturdays. Traffic patterns change a lot from day to day.

Will I learn jughandles in Paramus?

Yes. New Jersey's jughandles, where you exit right to turn left, are common along Route 17 and are covered in lessons for local drivers.

How do I book driving lessons in Paramus?
