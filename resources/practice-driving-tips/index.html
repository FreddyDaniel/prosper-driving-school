Practice Driving Tips for North Jersey Roads

How to make supervised practice time count: a progression plan from parking lots to Route 4, Route 17, the Garden State Parkway and I-80.

New Jersey's 50-hour practice requirement for drivers under 21 is a big commitment. Planned well, those hours turn a nervous permit holder into a confident driver. Here's a progression that fits Bergen County roads.

Stage 1: Control (hours 1–8)

Start in a large, empty lot or a quiet residential street (with permission where needed). Practice smooth acceleration, gentle braking, hand position, steering through turns, and backing straight. Keep sessions to 30–45 minutes.

Stage 2: Neighborhoods (hours 8–20)

Move to residential streets with stop signs and parked cars. Practice full stops, four-way-stop etiquette, scanning for children and cyclists, and right turns on red where allowed. Towns like Maywood, Bergenfield and Hasbrouck Heights are ideal.

Stage 3: Downtowns and arterials (hours 20–35)

Add Main Street-style downtowns (Hackensack, Englewood, Ridgewood) and busier roads like Kinderkamack Road or Teaneck Road. Practice lane changes, protected and unprotected left turns, jughandles and parallel parking.

Stage 4: Highways (hours 35–50)

Once control is automatic, practice merging on Route 4 and Route 17, then the Garden State Parkway and I-80 at off-peak times. Focus on matching speed, checking blind spots, keeping a safe following distance, and choosing lanes early for exits.

Night and weather hours

At least 10 of the 50 hours must be in darkness. Spread them out, and include rain when it's safe so your teen learns how headlights, glare and wet roads change things.

Coaching tips for supervising drivers

  • Give directions early and clearly: "At the next light, turn left."
  • Stay calm; praise good decisions as much as you correct mistakes.
  • Debrief after each drive with one strength and one thing to work on.
  • Log every session right away.

Not sure where to start? A few professional lessons early on give you a skills baseline and a plan.
