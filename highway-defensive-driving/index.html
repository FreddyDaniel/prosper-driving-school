Master the highway.
Drive defensively.

Highway skills

Bergen County drivers face short on-ramps on Route 17, jughandles on Route 4, toll plazas and fast traffic on the Garden State Parkway, and heavy trucks on I-80. We practice each of these deliberately, at off-peak times first.

  • Matching speed and merging smoothly
  • Lane selection, signage and early exit planning
  • Safe following distance and blind-spot checks
  • Sharing the road with trucks

Defensive driving habits

Defensive driving means anticipating others' mistakes: scanning far ahead, keeping an escape route, managing speed for conditions, and eliminating distractions. See our winter driving guide for seasonal tips.

FAQ

Questions we hear

Is this course the same as the NJ point-reduction Defensive Driving Program?

No. These are practical behind-the-wheel lessons. The MVC-approved Defensive Driving Program for point reduction is a separate classroom or online course.

Do I need to be licensed for highway lessons?

Permit holders and licensed drivers can both take highway lessons once they have solid basic control.

Which highways do you practice on?

Typically Route 4, Route 17, the Garden State Parkway and I-80, depending on your goals and skill level.
