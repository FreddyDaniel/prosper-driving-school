How old do you have to be to start driving lessons in New Jersey?

Teens can get a special learner permit at 16 by enrolling in an approved 6-hour behind-the-wheel course with a licensed driving school, with parent or guardian consent, and passing the knowledge and vision tests. Adults can start anytime.

How much do driving lessons cost?

Pricing depends on the package and your goals. Call 551-999-6131 or request a quote online and we'll recommend the right option.

Can I use your car for my road test?

Yes. Our road test package can include use of our vehicle and a warm-up lesson. Call to check availability for your test date.

What areas do you serve?

We're based at 131 Main Street, Suite 168 in Hackensack and serve Bergen County and North Jersey, including Teaneck, Paramus, Fort Lee, Englewood, Lodi, Fair Lawn, Ridgewood and more.

Do you teach nervous or anxious drivers?

Yes. We go at your pace, start on quiet streets, and explain everything before we do it.

Do you use a dual-control car?

Yes. Every lesson is in a dual-control vehicle.

What is New Jersey's 50-hour rule?

For permits issued on or after February 1, 2025, drivers under 21 must complete at least 50 hours of supervised practice driving, including 10 in darkness, certified on form BA-CSD, before a probationary license.

Where is the closest road test site?

For most Bergen County drivers, the NJ MVC road test site at 8 Mill St (off Garibaldi Ave) in Lodi. Wayne (481 Route 46 West) is another option.

What are your hours?

Please call 551-999-6131 for current hours and lesson availability.

Do you teach drivers with foreign licenses?

Yes. We help international license holders learn NJ rules and prepare for the knowledge and road tests the MVC may require.

How do I book?

Call 551-999-6131 or use our online booking form, and we'll call you back to confirm.

Ready to get behind the wheel?

Call us or request a lesson online and we'll help you choose the right plan.
