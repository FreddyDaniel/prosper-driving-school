FAQ

Common questions

How old do you have to be to start driving lessons in New Jersey?

Teens can get a special learner permit at 16 by enrolling in an approved 6-hour behind-the-wheel course with a licensed driving school, with parent or guardian consent, and passing the knowledge and vision tests. Adults can start anytime.

How much do driving lessons cost?

Pricing depends on the package and your goals. Call 551-999-6131 or request a quote online and we'll recommend the right option.

Can I use your car for my road test?

Yes. Our road test package can include use of our vehicle and a warm-up lesson. Call to check availability for your test date.

What areas do you serve?

We're based at 131 Main Street, Suite 168 in Hackensack and serve Bergen County and North Jersey, including Teaneck, Paramus, Fort Lee, Englewood, Lodi, Fair Lawn, Ridgewood and more.

Do you teach nervous or anxious drivers?

Yes. We go at your pace, start on quiet streets, and explain everything before we do it.
