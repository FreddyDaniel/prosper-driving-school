What NJ requires

The NJ Driver Manual states that all out-of-country applicants must pass the knowledge test and vision screening and may be required to pass a road test. The MVC lists reciprocity agreements with Taiwan and South Korea that can exempt eligible adults from testing. Visitors may drive up to one year with a foreign license and an International Driving Permit.

FAQ

Questions we hear

Do I have to take the road test?

Out-of-country applicants may be required to take a road test. MVC staff decide after reviewing your documents.

Can I use your car for the road test?

Yes, our road test package can include our vehicle. Call to check availability.

Which countries have reciprocity with NJ?

The NJ MVC currently lists the Republic of China (Taiwan) and the Republic of Korea. Check the MVC for updates.
