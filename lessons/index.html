Questions

About our lessons

Do you list prices online?

Pricing depends on the package, scheduling and your goals, so we quote each student directly. Call 551-999-6131 or request a quote online.

How long is a lesson?

Lesson length depends on the package. We'll confirm when you book.

Do lessons use a dual-control car?

Yes. NJ's required teen course must be taught in a dual-controlled vehicle, and we use dual controls for every lesson so you can learn safely.

Ready to get behind the wheel?

Call us or request a lesson online and we'll help you choose the right plan.
